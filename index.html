Scope and unresolved questions

The mathematical argument assumes valid interval enclosures and complete enumeration. The tool counts equilibria inside a declared state box and classifies linear stability; it makes no claim about periodic orbits or attraction basins.

The historical archive contains 21 models, 22,173 cells and 11,035 records labelled certified. These are reanalysed stored results, not a fresh run of the repaired code. CAPD, Z3 and dReal archive results likewise remain historical.

Validated root solving and parameter-uniform existence/uniqueness are established methods. IBEX documentation describes the closest mechanism overlap. A distinct advance requires task-matched comparison and a consequential biological decision.
